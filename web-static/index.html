Quick Beginner Guide & Common Questions

1. How and when do I get paid?

While mining, you earn Shares (proof of your CPU's work over the last 24 hours). Whenever anyone on P2Pool solves a full 50 SCASH block, the Scash blockchain immediately pays 99% directly into active miners' wallets and 1% (0.50 SCASH) to the pool author. Nothing is held back on a server!

2. How do I get maximum CPU mining speed?

Click Yes on the Windows Administrator prompt when starting Start-Scash-P2Pool.exe so the built-in WinRing0x64.sys MSR speed booster turns on. Restart your PC once after your very first run so Windows finishes unlocking HugePages memory!

3. Using Scash Wallet on the same PC?

P2Pool automatically finds your saved Scash Wallet blockchain in AppData\Local\Scash so you don't download blocks twice! If Scash Wallet was already open before your first run, restart Scash Wallet once (or close it before starting P2Pool) so they share smoothly.

4. How do I stop mining cleanly?

Click Stop Built-in XMRig Miner on the Overview tab to pause just the CPU miner, or simply close the black terminal window (X button) to shut down the node, pool, and miner all at once with nothing left running in Task Manager.
